## Grosmont geometry

The Grosmont geometry is derived from the segmented digital-rock benchmark attributed to Andrä et al. in the accompanying manuscript. The repository contains the 400³ crop and 192³ resampling used in the paper. These data retain the original dataset's attribution and applicable terms.

## Research materials

The paper's measurements, input records and source snapshots are stored in `repro/raw/`. Their source-to-result mapping is provided in `repro/table_manifest.json` and `repro/path_map.json`.
